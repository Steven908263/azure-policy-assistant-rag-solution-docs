# Password Reset Guide

**Company:** Northstar Digital Services, Inc.  
**Document Owner:** IT Service Desk  
**Document ID:** GUIDE-IT-020  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how employees and contractors reset their corporate password and recover access to Northstar Digital Services, Inc. systems. It is intended for standard user account recovery and does not apply to privileged administrator accounts, service accounts, or emergency security lockouts.

## 2. When to Use This Guide

Use this guide when:

- You forgot your password.
- Your password expired.
- You received a prompt to change your password.
- You can sign in but want to change your password proactively.
- The Service Desk asked you to complete a self-service password reset.

Do not use this guide if you suspect your account was compromised. Instead, contact the IT Service Desk immediately and report a potential security incident.

## 3. Password Requirements

Passwords must meet the following standards:

- Minimum length: 14 characters.
- Must not contain your name, username, company name, or common dictionary phrases.
- Must be unique and not reused from other personal or business accounts.
- Must not be shared with coworkers, managers, vendors, family members, or IT support.
- Must be changed immediately if you believe it has been exposed.

A passphrase such as `BlueRiver!TrainWindow92` is easier to remember and stronger than a short complex password.

## 4. Self-Service Password Reset Steps

1. Go to the company sign-in page from a trusted browser.
2. Select **Forgot password** or **Can’t access your account**.
3. Enter your company email address.
4. Complete multi-factor authentication using your enrolled method.
5. Choose a new password that meets the password requirements.
6. Confirm the new password.
7. Sign out of all active sessions when prompted.
8. Sign back in using the new password.

## 5. After Resetting Your Password

After your password is changed, update any signed-in company applications on your laptop, phone, or tablet. Common applications include:

- Email and calendar.
- VPN or secure access client.
- Collaboration tools.
- Password manager.
- Developer tools and command-line credentials.
- Mobile device mail profile, if approved for company use.

## 6. Multi-Factor Authentication Issues

If you cannot complete MFA:

1. Confirm that your phone has internet or cellular service.
2. Try an alternate enrolled MFA method, if available.
3. Check whether your authenticator app time sync is enabled.
4. Contact the IT Service Desk if your device was replaced, lost, stolen, or reset.

The Service Desk will verify your identity before changing MFA methods.

## 7. Locked Accounts

Your account may lock temporarily after too many failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or contact the IT Service Desk if the issue continues. Repeated lockouts may indicate a saved old password on another device or application.

## 8. Security Reminders

- IT will never ask you to disclose your password.
- Do not approve MFA prompts you did not initiate.
- Report suspicious password reset emails, unexpected MFA prompts, or unusual account activity.
- Use the company-approved password manager for work credentials.
- Do not store passwords in notes, spreadsheets, browsers without approval, or chat messages.

## 9. Service Desk Escalation

Contact the IT Service Desk when:

- Self-service reset fails.
- You lost access to all MFA methods.
- You suspect account compromise.
- Your account is locked repeatedly.
- A privileged or service account password needs to be changed.

When contacting support, provide your name, department, callback number, device type, and a brief description of the issue. Do not include your current or previous password.

## 10. Audit and Compliance

Password reset events, MFA changes, and Service Desk identity verification records may be logged for security, compliance, and audit purposes. Logs are retained according to the company retention schedule.
